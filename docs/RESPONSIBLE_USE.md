# Responsible Use

This project is designed for legitimate research, local-market analysis, data-quality work, and demonstrations of geospatial engineering.

## Prohibited or unsupported uses

Do not use or extend it to:

- bypass CAPTCHAs, authentication, robots controls, or technical access restrictions;
- hide automation through identity rotation or anti-detection techniques;
- scrape the Google Maps website directly;
- harvest sensitive personal data;
- conduct harassment, stalking, surveillance, discrimination, spam, or deceptive outreach;
- resell provider data when the provider's terms do not permit it;
- treat unverified records as authoritative.

## OpenStreetMap

OpenStreetMap data has its own attribution and database-license requirements. Public Nominatim and Overpass servers are shared community resources, not free unlimited production infrastructure. Keep traffic low, identify the application truthfully, cache where appropriate, and use dedicated infrastructure for sustained use.

## Google Places

The optional Google adapter uses the official Places API (New), not browser scraping. Users are responsible for enabling the service, securing their key, selecting fields, understanding billing, and complying with current Google Maps Platform terms, display requirements, attribution rules, and retention restrictions.

## Contact data

Phone numbers and email addresses can be personal data. Collect only what is necessary, establish a lawful basis, define retention periods, honor deletion and objection requests, and comply with privacy and marketing law applicable to the user and recipient.

## AI analysis

Model output may be wrong. The prompt instructs the model to use only supplied records and mark uncertainty, but users must still verify material conclusions.
